REGIS MARIE COLLEGE - ENROLLMENT & ACADEMIC LOAD POLICIES

1. Regular and Late Enrollment Procedures:
Regular enrollment occurs during the designated schedule published by the Registrar before each semester. 
- Late Enrollment: A student is considered a late enrollee if they register after the official start of classes. A non-refundable Late Enrollment Fee of PHP 500.00 will be added to the student account.
- No student is permitted to enroll in any course after the second week of regular classes.

2. Maximum and Minimum Academic Load:
- Regular Semesters: The standard load is between 15 to 21 units per semester, as defined by the curriculum program checklist.
- Overloading: Students with a General Weighted Average (GWA) of 1.75 or higher in the previous semester may request an overload of up to 3 additional units (max 24 units), subject to the approval of the Department Dean and the Registrar.
- Summer Term: The maximum load is 9 units. Overloading is not permitted during summer terms.

3. Adding and Dropping of Courses:
Students may add or drop courses within the first two weeks of the semester:
- First Week of Classes: 100% tuition refund is applicable for dropped courses (excluding registration fees).
- Second Week of Classes: 50% tuition refund is applicable for dropped courses.
- After Second Week of Classes: Dropping is permitted up to the midterm week, but 0% refund is issued. The grade of "W" (Withdrawn) will be recorded on the student's transcript.
- Unauthorized Dropping: Students who stop attending a class without submitting an official Dropping Form will receive a grade of "5.0" (Failed).

4. Prerequisite Policy:
Students are strictly prohibited from enrolling in subjects where the prerequisite courses have not been passed. Any enrollment in a subject without passing its prerequisite will be invalidated and will receive no credit, regardless of class performance.
