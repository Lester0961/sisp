REGIS MARIE COLLEGE - DOCUMENT REQUEST POLICIES & PROCEDURES

1. Official Transcript of Records (TOR):
Students may request their Official Transcript of Records (TOR) from the Office of the Registrar. 
- Processing time: 7 to 10 working days from the date of payment and clearance clearance approval.
- Fee: PHP 200.00 per page.
- Requirements: Completed Document Request Form, fully signed clearance from the Accounting Office, Library, and Dean's Office, and a valid student photo ID.

2. Certificate of Enrollment / Registration (COE):
Used to prove active enrollment status for scholarships, health insurance, or external subsidies.
- Processing time: 3 working days.
- Fee: PHP 75.00.
- Requirements: Current semester registration form or proof of matriculation.

3. Certificate of Graduation (COG):
For alumni seeking proof of graduation prior to receiving the physical diploma.
- Processing time: 5 working days.
- Fee: PHP 150.00.
- Requirements: Graduation clearance approval and fully settled accounts.

4. Honorable Dismissal / Transfer Credentials:
Required when a student wishes to transfer to another academic institution.
- Processing time: 7 working days.
- Fee: PHP 250.00.
- Note: Issuance of transfer credentials means the student's status at Regis Marie College is officially deactivated.

General Ordering and Delivery Policy:
All document requests must be filed online through the Student Information and Services Portal (SISP) or in person at the Registrar counter. Expedited requests are not available. Documents can be picked up in person (with valid ID) or delivered via authorized couriers (additional delivery fees apply). If a representative is picking up the document, a signed authorization letter and copies of valid IDs for both the student and representative are strictly required.
