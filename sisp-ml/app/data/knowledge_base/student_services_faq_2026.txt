4. Have the new class schedule encoded and validated at the
Registrar's Office.

Topic: GRADING / INC PROCEDURE
Q: What is the procedure for processing an INC form according
to the February 2026 Registrar memo?
A:
Step 1 — Payment
• Proceed to the Treasury Office and pay the required fee
for the INC form.
• Retain the official receipt.
Step 2 — Compliance Check
• Return to the Dean's Office with the receipt and all other
required documents.
• The Dean's Office will verify compliance and confirm that
the requirements are complete.
Step 3 — Submission
• Obtain the Dean's signature on the INC form after
verification.
• Submit the fully completed and signed form to the Records
Department.

Office of the Registrar
