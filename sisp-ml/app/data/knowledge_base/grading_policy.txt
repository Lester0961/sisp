REGIS MARIE COLLEGE - GRADING & ACADEMIC STANDINGS POLICY

1. The Grading Scale:
Regis Marie College uses a numerical grading system to evaluate student performance:
- 1.00: 97 - 100% (Excellent / Outstanding)
- 1.25: 94 - 96% (Superior)
- 1.50: 91 - 93% (Very Good)
- 1.75: 88 - 90% (Good)
- 2.00: 85 - 87% (Satisfactory)
- 2.25: 82 - 84% (Fair)
- 2.50: 79 - 81% (Passed / Average)
- 2.75: 76 - 78% (Low Pass)
- 3.00: 75% (Passing Grade)
- 5.00: Below 75% (Failed)
- INC: Incomplete (Missing major requirements or exams)
- W: Officially Withdrawn

2. Resolution of Incomplete (INC) Grades:
A grade of INC is temporarily given if a student fails to submit a major final requirement or miss the final examination due to justifiable reasons.
- Timeframe: The student must resolve the INC grade by completing the missing requirements or exam within one (1) academic year from the end of the term in which the grade was received.
- Consequence of Non-resolution: If the student fails to resolve the INC within the one-year window, the INC automatically converts to a grade of "5.00" (Failed).

3. Grade Appeals and Re-evaluation:
Students who believe a mathematical or clerical error occurred in computing their final grade may submit an appeal.
- Timeline: The formal written appeal must be submitted to the Dean's Office within five (5) working days from the date of final grade posting in the SISP.
- Process: The Dean will review the appeal alongside the course instructor. If approved, the grade change form is signed by the Dean, Instructor, and Registrar. No grade change is accepted after the five-day appeal period.

4. Academic Probation and Dismissal:
- Probation: Any student who fails in two or more subjects (receiving a grade of 5.00) in a single semester will be placed on Academic Probation and will be restricted to a maximum load of 15 units in the next term.
- Dismissal: Accumulating three or more failing grades (5.00) or failing to lift probation status in the subsequent term may result in academic dismissal from Regis Marie College.
